### 2. Derivative Kernel Fusion
Currently, `_gradient` and `_laplacian` are separate method calls that trigger independent `torch.fft` or `torch.roll` operations.

* **The Fix:** For **Spectral Mode**, combine these into a single `_compute_derivatives` call. Since both $\nabla$ and $\nabla^2$ require the same `FFT(f)`, we should compute the FFT once, multiply by both wavenumber arrays ($ik$ and $-k^2$), and then perform two IFFTs.
* **Impact:** Reduces FFT compute cost by ~33% per RHS evaluation.

### 3. In-Place "Buffer" Management
The current `step()` method creates multiple clones (`rho.clone()`, `S.clone()`) and temporary $k1, k2, k3, k4$ tensors. At high qubit counts ($N > 14$), these allocations trigger expensive GPU memory defragmentation.

* **The Fix:** Pre-allocate static buffers for $k1 \dots k4$ and the intermediate RK4 states during `__init__`.
* **Impact:** Moves the `HamiltonianEvolver` from being **memory-allocation bound** to being **compute bound**, significantly reducing the 59ms latency you observed in field evaluation.

* **The Fix:** Use the `_use_real_active` logic to skip complex casts during the `energy()` calculation if the state is purely real.
